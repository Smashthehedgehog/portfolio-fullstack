Well now, it's been two whole years since I last wrote an article on my portfolio. Notice something different about the website? I hope so. After all, it has been completely revamped! The Navigation Bar is now on the top like most websites, the UI/UX looks a bit more modern, and the 'About Me' page is now a graph! Some may say that this website feels less professional, and that I'm not going to attract as many recruiters that way. That's fine. That's not necessarily the direction I want to take with this website. Instead, I want to make my website stand out a little more, invoke more of my own creativity, and be a bit of a playground where I can exhibit some of my projects, artifacts, and strengths while still showing the world that I'M ME.

It will be similar to how I journal in my Notes whenever I feel overwhelmed or compelled to express my feelings...except it won't be that sort of journaling. That's why those thoughts STAY in the Notes.

But that's all I wanted to update everyone who reads this article on! Some of these articles will be pretty technical, some will be me just rambling, it all depends on what I want to talk about. Best believe however, I will do a better job with these articles!
