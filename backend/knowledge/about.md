# About Michael Ani

## Overview
Michael Chibuikem Ani is a huge Sonic the Hedgehog fan and a full-stack web developer (React, Bootstrap, JavaScript) with a data science edge — building everything from AI-powered tools and neural networks to full CRUD applications and automated data workflows in Python and SQL.

## Tech & Coding Journey
Michael got his acceptance letter to Georgia Tech in January 2020. He initially enrolled as a Math major, but after his first coding class decided he wanted to be more involved in software engineering, and picked up a Computer Science minor from there. Since Georgia Tech, he's kept up with his dev skills, leveraging AI and focusing more on architecture. He worked as a Data Engineer at Northside Hospital, and now works as a Software Engineer at VISA.

## Powerlifting
Michael has kept up with his gym progress and compound lifts since high school, but didn't start deadlifting until late 2023. After college he kept training generally until he decided to start competitive powerlifting in August 2025. He competes in the USAPL federation (chosen for being more beginner-friendly; he's considering USA Powerlifting/PA for its more competitive field). His biggest highlight so far was a strong performance at the State Championships; his toughest outing was at Nationals. Current PRs: Squat 606 lbs, Bench 402 lbs, Deadlift 772 lbs.

## Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity
Michael crossed into Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity, Inc. through the Nu Mu chapter at Georgia Institute of Technology in Spring 2023, as the tenth member ("Big 10") in a line of 13. He counts crossing as one of the best things he did at Georgia Tech, and has made many strong connections with brothers since.

## Nigerian & Igbo Heritage
Michael was born and raised in the United States; both of his parents are Nigerian and Igbo. His father is from Enugu State and his mother is from Abia State. He doesn't speak Igbo fluently yet but can understand some of it.

He's followed Sonic-related content of all kinds since — fan animations, custom sprite comic series, the Archie and IDW Sonic comics, and of course the games — through the franchise's ups and downs since the mid-2000s.
